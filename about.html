A.

Briefing

Process

We jump on a call (or chat, whatever works for you) and talk through your project, goals, and audience. I ask all the right questions to figure out what you really need and how I can help. Super easygoing, no boring stuff.

Result

I get a clear picture of what you need. You get a sense of how I work.
Sounds like win-win.
After this stage I'll put together a tailored proposal with the scope, timeline, and budget. No surprises, no hidden fees.

B.

Discovery

Process

I do my homework. Dig into your industry, check out your competitors, and look closely at your target audience. If you have any extra materials, I'll ask for them too. The goal here is to get the full picture and make smart decisions for the project.

Result

We'll have a solid strategy for your website and a clear direction. No guesswork — just a focused plan that makes sense.

C.

UX stage

Process

If the project calls for it (e.g. the project has a lot of pages), I create the information architecture: what pages we need, how they connect, and how everything flows. I also do any extra research to make sure the website delivers on its core function — not just looking amazing, but actually working for your business.

Result

We get a clear structure for your website that makes it easy for
people to navigate and get where they need to go. We all know that solid foundation = smooth experience.

D.

Design stage

Process

First, we lock in the visual direction together - making sure the vibe and style hit the mark. Once that's set, I move on to design. Thanks to my experience, I create high-level work that doesn't just look good, but leaves a lasting impression. It's all about combining function with that wow factor. I keep you always updated, so you're never out of the loop.

Result

You get a design that not only works for your audience but also makes your brand stand out. It's functional, beautiful, and makes people say, "Damn, that's cool. I want their product!"

E.

Development

Process

If you need a full launch, I'll take care of everything – from animations to responsive layouts, fully built out on Webflow or custom code. Already working with developers? That's cool too – I'll prep all the files, coordinate with them directly, and make sure everything's crystal clear.
End goal's the same: a top-notch final result.

Result

Your website goes live, works flawlessly, and looks exactly how we planned it. We're happy, and your users get a killer experience.
