Quantum Tunneling Through Non-Standard Barriers

A 1D finite-difference Schrödinger equation solver written in pure Python (zero external dependencies). Solves tridiagonal Hamiltonian eigenvalues using Sturm sequence bisection and simulates time-dependent wave-packet collisions via Crank-Nicolson. Systematically examined how barrier curvature (exponential, Gaussian, trapezoidal) alters transmission by orders of magnitude compared to standard rectangular textbook models.

Philosophy Without Exaggeration

Notes & Principles
THE "UNDERGROUND" INERTIA

Overcoming Analytical Paralysis

Dostoevsky's Notes from Underground illustrates the danger of acute self-consciousness: hyper-analyzing everything until you cannot act. You cannot think your way out of overthinking. The only real antidote is physical grounding—daily physical training, lifting, and shipping functional code. When in doubt, the body acts.

CONSUMPTION TO ACTION

No Fluff, Just Practice

Reading articles and books without immediate practical execution is passive entertainment masquerading as work. Every piece of consumed knowledge must be distilled into a clear, verifiable action, script, or habit within a defined time limit.

THE LAW OF 33%

Grounded Calibration

Spend 33% of time with mentors and books who push you far past comfort; 33% with peers on the same level to collaborate with; and 33% teaching and simplifying ideas for those beginning. It preserves humility while preventing complacency.

GITA & STOIC REFLECTION

Execution Without Hype

Constant attachment to external outcome, praise, or vanity creates anxiety. Grounding oneself in duty (doing the work with complete technical rigor without needing to brag) is the most sustainable way to build longevity.
